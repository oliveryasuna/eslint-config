## Overlays

Path-scoped rule relaxations. Opt-in via `overlays: COMMON_OVERLAYS`; none
are applied by default.

| Overlay | Files | Rules relaxed | Rationale |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `dts` | `**/*.d.?([cm])ts` | 4 | Declaration files are frequently generated and legitimately contain duplicate imports, unused type-only bindings, and blanket disables. |
| `cjs` | `**/*.cjs` | 2 | A .cjs file is CommonJS by definition; flagging require() there is noise. |
